# Asset provenance and reuse

Prepared 31 October 2026 for the user's Dima learning-planner identity package.

## Logo

The user approved the original Arabic wordmark plus soft-step book icon in the attached proof screenshot. Vector masters are source-faithful production reconstructions by the assistant from the previously generated artwork. They are not font substitutions and not proof of trademark clearance or exclusive rights. Preserve the supplied source, source map and fidelity record under `assets/source/`.

The Arabic name was retained by explicit user choice after a same-name adjacent service had been disclosed. No domain, affiliation or legal registration is claimed. The Latin string `dima` in filenames is a technical identifier; no new Latin wordmark is silently approved.

## Fonts

Manrope and Noto Sans Arabic are bundled from official Google Fonts sources under their accompanying SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts. Keep the license files when redistributing the fonts. Binary URLs, retrieval date and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `assets/fonts/manifest.json`. Variable TTF files support native/editor use; web WOFF2 subsets are separate files. The approved Arabic wordmark does not use these as a replaceable font.

## Interface icons, geometric motifs and templates

Locally authored SVG UI geometry, source files and templates are delivered for the user's project. Any reused third-party icon library must be separately identified in the relevant source README; do not infer a license for an unidentified library. No paid stock image, premium component, hosted font subscription or purchased service is required by this package.

## Product names and demo content

Google Project Management and CS50 Business appear as externally hosted course examples, not endorsements or partnerships. No provider logo is supplied as Dima-owned artwork. Sample learner names, ranks, sessions, percentages and plan dates are design demonstration data, not observed users or business metrics. Replace them before publishing.

## Screenshots and exports

Prototype screenshots are captures of the supplied local UI, not photographs of a live service. PNG/PDF exports derive from the same masters/source files. Editable SVG templates may require installing the bundled fonts in the editing application. Browser previews load the self-hosted font files. Check the actual edited export before publication.
